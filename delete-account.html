Bachat AI – Delete your account and data

App: Bachat AI (com.bhavan.bachat_ai) on Google Play · Last updated: 26 September 2026

In short: open Bachat AI → More → Delete all data → Yes, delete. Your account and everything in it are deleted from our servers straight away. No app? Email us and we do it within 7 days.

Option 1 – from the app (immediate)

  1. Open Bachat AI.
  2. Tap More in the bottom bar.
  3. Scroll to Your data and tap Delete all data.
  4. Tap Yes, delete.

Option 2 – without the app

Email login2atharv53@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Bachat AI account”. If you backed up with Google, send it from that Google account’s email so we can find your data. We delete it within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your account, including the email address and name of your Google account if you backed up with Google
  • All transactions, alerts and reminders the app worked out from your SMS
  • Your bills, EMIs, premiums, fees, credit cards (last 4 digits) and goals
  • Lending entries, vehicles and payment disputes
  • Your notification token

What is kept

Nothing. We keep no copy and no backup of your data after deletion. Your SMS and photos were never on our servers in the first place – they stay on your phone, and uninstalling the app removes anything the app stored on the phone.

If you signed in with Google you can also remove Bachat AI’s access at myaccount.google.com/connections.
