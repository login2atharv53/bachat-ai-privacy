Bachat AI – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026 · App package: com.bhavan.bachat_ai

In one line: Bachat AI reads your bank and UPI SMS on your phone to track spending, bills, challans and insurance dates. Your SMS messages are never uploaded, sold or shared. You can delete everything from inside the app at any time.

1. Who we are

Bachat AI is a personal finance assistant for Android, published by Bhavan Classes, Sector 15, Chandigarh, India. Contact: login2atharv53@gmail.com.

2. What the app does with SMS

The core purpose of Bachat AI is SMS-based money management. With your permission (READ_SMS and RECEIVE_SMS), the app reads messages on your device to detect:

  • UPI and bank transaction messages (amount, merchant, date)
  • Low-balance alerts from your bank
  • Bill due-date messages (electricity, gas, mobile, broadband, credit card)
  • Traffic challan messages
  • Insurance renewal reminders

All SMS reading and parsing happens on your device. Messages that are not financial (OTPs, personal chats, promotions) are ignored and never stored.

The app works without SMS permission too – you can add entries manually. Nothing in the app requires the permission except automatic detection.

2a. Payment notifications from UPI apps (optional)

Card and bank SMS often do not name who you paid. If you switch on Payment names from UPI apps (Android notification access), the app reads payment notifications from UPI apps only – such as PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm, BHIM and Amazon Pay – to find the payee’s name and put it on the matching payment.

  • Notifications from every other app (WhatsApp, messages, email and so on) are ignored and never read.
  • Only notifications that mention an amount are kept, on your phone only, and deleted after 3 days.
  • Only the payee name is saved with the payment. The notification itself is never uploaded.
  • You can switch it off at any time in Android settings or in the app under More.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not upload your full SMS inbox. Only messages identified as financial are processed, and non-financial messages are discarded immediately.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your data with any third party.
  • We do not show advertisements.
  • We do not collect your contacts, location or call logs, and we do not read notifications from any app other than UPI payment apps (and only if you switch that on).
  • Photos you take to fill in a form (a card, a bill, a policy) are read on your phone and deleted straight after. The photo is never uploaded or stored, and a full card number, expiry date or CVV is never kept – only the few details the form needs, which you check before saving.
  • We do not send SMS on your behalf. Reminder messages open your own WhatsApp or SMS app with a pre-filled draft that you choose to send.

5. Third-party services

The app uses Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Messaging) to store your data and deliver notifications, Google Sign-In if you choose to back up with Google, and Google ML Kit text recognition, which runs entirely on your phone, to read photos you take. Google's handling of this infrastructure is governed by the Firebase Privacy and Security terms and the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Deleting your data

Open the app → More → Delete all data. This permanently deletes all your transactions, reminders, bills, goals, cards, lending entries, vehicles and your account from our servers immediately. If you signed in with Google, you can also remove Bachat AI's access at myaccount.google.com/connections. You may also email us at the address above and we will delete your data within 7 days. Step-by-step instructions: Delete your account and data.

Uninstalling the app removes all local data. Revoking SMS permission in Android settings stops all SMS reading instantly.

7. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored in Google Firebase with per-user access rules. No employee or third party has routine access to your records.

8. Children

Bachat AI is intended for adults managing their own finances and is not directed at children under 18.
